# Release 2026-11-21

The first release under the release guide (`docs/releasing.md`). This site has no version
numbers and no change log: this dated record is the release's note. It is the batch-a work,
audited in the tenth audit (#161) and merged into `dev` through !160.

## What changed for visitors

- The front door loads more smoothly. Its pictures arrive in small sizes first and larger
  ones ease in as they download, so the door shows something sooner on a slow connection
  (#148, #119). If a larger picture fails to download, the door now asks for it once more
  instead of staying blurry for the whole visit (#164).
- The sun is painted off the main thread, and the galaxy is a ready-made picture, so the
  door stays responsive while it loads (#119).
- A planet's name no longer shows before the door has been revealed, so it cannot sit over
  an empty, still-loading door (#157).
- The language links keep your choice in the address, and a reload on another household's
  sky shows that sky (#138).
- The door no longer measures the dive on load (#155) and gives up on a stalled flight
  world cleanly instead of leaving its check open (#138).
- The sign-in and goodbye pages now switch to their phone layout under 840px wide, not
  560px (ADR-0003 section 3c, amended 2026-11-19; Orbit matches it).

## Behind the scenes

- The release guide, this record's template and a readiness check now exist (#156).
- Translations of the docs are produced by a reviewed, bounded pipeline (#125, #158).
- Flaky browser tests were fixed or logged, and coverage floors were raised (#164, #162).
- The lint now refuses any tracked file name containing a newline, and the galaxy rule
  judges every tracked name (#162).
- Freeze check 5 (`?gpu=force`, Chromium) failed at random three times, so it is
  quarantined: it still runs and logs its result but no longer fails the merge gate, until
  #165 (freeze 5 flake) fixes or rewrites it. It is listed in `docs/flakes.md`.
