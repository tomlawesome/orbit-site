A tomlawson.io project

orbit

Everything in your orbit, on track.

A self-hosted home operations hub for maintenance, renewals, recurring services, contracts, cover, and the people who share them.

scroll
The orbit

Your home has an orbit.

Boilers need servicing. Insurance renews. Cars need inspections. Contracts roll over.

Orbit puts them all round one sun: your household. The nearer something is, the sooner it's due. Done, and it swings back out to its next date. Nothing slips past unseen.

The rhythm

Done once. Remembered for good.

Complete it, and the next date is worked out for you.

Every item keeps its own rhythm: when it's due, how often it comes round, what it costs, when you'll be reminded, and the documents that go with it. Reschedule or snooze when life gets in the way.

The relay

Forward, then file.

Every household has its own relay address.

Forward a bill or a policy to it. Orbit reads the attachment, works out what it is, and asks before anything is added. Your mail stays in your mailbox; Orbit only ever reads a copy.

Around your household

Shared, private, and yours to shape.

AJM

Share the load

Add the people you live with: by name if they already have an Orbit account, or by an invitation sent to their email.

Sections that fit

Home, Vehicles, Devices and Services to begin with. Add, rename, reorder and recolour your own.

Make it yours

Five themes, four text sizes.

A history for everything

What was done and when, reminders before it's due, and its documents, stored encrypted.

On your home screen

Installs like an app and can notify you. Your data stays on your server, not copied onto the phone.

Private by design

Your own sign-in, or the identity provider you already use. Nothing is shared beyond your household.

The system

One app. Standard parts.

Three containers on a Linux machine of your own, set up by one command. Nothing exotic to learn, nothing to babysit.

You bring
A Linux host with Docker and a domain with HTTPS. Sign in with Orbit's own accounts, or with a provider you already use (any OpenID Connect, such as Authentik) if you would rather.
It runs
Orbit itself, PostgreSQL 18 for your records, and ClamAV to check every upload before it is kept. That is all it needs. ClamAV wants about 4 GB of memory of its own; an administrator can turn scanning off.
If you like
Email reminders, push notifications to your phone, and an inbox you forward documents to.
Your data
On your own disk: records in the database, documents encrypted at rest.
Updates
Run the installer again. Every release is pinned to an exact image, and your settings carry over.
Yours, all the way down

No paywall. No plan.
No account with us.

Orbit runs on your own server and asks nothing of you: no subscription, and no login anywhere but your own front door. Nothing is held back for a higher tier, because there isn't one. The source is open, the licence is the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later, and it stays that way.
