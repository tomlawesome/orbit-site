Everything in your orbit, on track.

Boilers need servicing. Insurance renews. Cars need inspections. Devices leave warranty. Orbit brings those scattered responsibilities into one calm, shared view, on a server you run.

Your home, drawn as a year. Each item is a planet; its position on the dial is when it's next due.

Why Orbit

Your home has an orbit

Contracts roll over. Cover lapses. Services come round again. Orbit keeps the important things visible before they become urgent, for everyone who shares the place.

◆

See what is next

An urgency-aware view of what's coming, what's overdue and what was just done. The dial shows the year; the list shows what needs attention this month.

↻

Keep the rhythm

Complete, renew, reschedule, snooze, cancel or restore an item, and Orbit works out the next date for anything that recurs.

✦

Share the load

Households have members. Add people who already use your Orbit, hand items over, and keep more than one household in the same sky.

A tour

Around the sky

Screens from the current development line, showing synthetic household data. Select one to enlarge it.

Designed around your household

Built for the things a home actually has

Sections that fit your life

Home, Vehicles, Devices and Services come as defaults. Add, rename, reorder or hide sections to match the place you live in.

A complete record of care

Schedule history, activity, archived records, reminders and the supporting documents, encrypted at rest and scanned before they're stored.

More than one household

A cottage, a relative's flat, the boat. Each household is its own system in the sky, with its own members and its own orbit.

Reminders that reach you

Email reminders and browser notifications, with a first warning and a final one, timed to how close each item is.

Private by design

Local password accounts are always available. Add an identity provider with OpenID Connect if you have one. Nothing is exposed to an unauthenticated visitor.

Yours to keep

Backups and restores are part of the product, with a recovery bundle for the keys. Your household's data does not depend on a hosted service.

Under the hood

One app. Standard supporting services.

Orbit deliberately keeps the operational footprint small. There is no custom database image and no separate frontend and backend to maintain.

Core orbit

The complete application: interface, authenticated APIs, migrations and the reminder scheduler, in one container.

Core orbit-postgres

The official PostgreSQL 18 image, pinned by digest, with a persistent volume.

Core document volume

Supporting documents are written as ciphertext only. The key never leaves the host.

Core orbit-clamav

The official scanner, fed a quarantined stream on a private network. No host port, no credentials, no documents.

Optional OIDC provider

Sign in with an identity provider you already run, alongside local accounts.

Optional SMTP · Web Push

Where reminders go.

Optional Tika · Ollama

Local document text extraction and a local model server, off by default and never required.

Install

One line, then a guided install

On a Linux host with Docker Compose, one command downloads a signed manifest, verifies the launcher and installer against it, and only then hands over. The launcher walks you through the rest and shows every step as it runs.
